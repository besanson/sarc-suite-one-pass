One Gate Is Not Enough: Non-Compensatory Composition of Pre-Action Controls for Agentic AI

Category suggestion
--------------------
Primary: cs.AI (Artificial Intelligence)
  Rationale: the paper's central contribution is the composition semantics of
  multiple pre-action gates governing an agentic AI system's actions --
  a join-semilattice model, a remediate-and-regate protocol, and their
  soundness/completeness properties -- squarely an AI-systems and
  formal-semantics contribution.

Cross-list: cs.SE (Software Engineering)
  Rationale: the artifact is a deterministic, open-data reference
  implementation with pre-registered hypotheses, exhaustive finite-model
  checkers, a 30-seed statistical sweep, and machine-checked/human-reviewed
  proof-status tagging for every claim -- an engineering-methodology
  contribution (empirical software engineering rigor) as much as a
  theoretical one.

Cross-list: cs.CY (Computers and Society)
  Rationale: the paper's subject is governance of agentic AI systems --
  authority, resource, and evidence-validity controls, and the honest
  reporting of what composition does and does not cover -- directly
  relevant to AI governance and accountability.
